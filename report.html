The Black Oasis: Umm al-Jimal

Mafraq Governorate, northern Jordan, about 17 km east of Mafraq

World Heritage Site no. 1721, inscribed July 2024, Jordan 7th site

  • 1st c. CE

    Nabataean roots

    Farming homesteads and a trading outpost serving Bostra.

  • 106 CE

    Roman rule

    Rome annexes the region. After 275 CE a fortified garrison is built.

  • 5th-6th c.

    Byzantine prosperity

    Town grows into farming and trading centre with about 6000 people and 16 churches.

  • 640 CE

    Early Islamic period

    Umayyad era: buildings are reused, houses become mosques, population shrinks.

  • c. 749 CE

    Earthquake and decline

    Major earthquake damages the town. Settlement ends by late 8th century.

  • 2024

    World Heritage Site

    Inscribed by UNESCO for authentic basalt architecture.
